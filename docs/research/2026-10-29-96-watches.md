# Ninety-six watches

How much memory kausa uses, and how long it takes to be ready, when it watches 32 namespaces on
their own with the Deployment watch on: 96 watches and 96 connections to the API server, against
the chart's memory limit of 128 MiB. Measured on 2026-10-29 as the condition of shipping `D014`
([0018. Explain a Deployment past its progress deadline (`D014`) from a Deployment watch that is
on by default, and add nothing for StatefulSets or DaemonSets](../adr/0018-stuck-rollouts.md)),
which would have lowered the cap of 32 namespaces to 16 if kausa had not stayed well under the
limit. It repeats [Thirty-two namespaces](2026-10-28-32-namespaces.md) (64 watches) with the same
objects and settings.

Once per scope, in a kind cluster on the maintainer's machine: a first data point, not a benchmark.

## Result

| | `rbac.scope=cluster` (3 watches) | `rbac.scope=namespaced`, 32 namespaces (96 watches) |
|---|---|---|
| Ready (`/readyz` 200) after the `started` line | 0.69 s | 0.60 s |
| Ready after `helm install` returned (scheduling and container start included) | 5.7 s | 5.2 s |
| Memory when ready (working set) | 4.9 MiB | 12.2 MiB |
| Memory in steady state, 15 s to 3 min after ready (working set) | 5.1 to 5.9 MiB | 11.9 to 13.5 MiB |
| Highest memory since the container started (`memory.peak`) | 14.1 MiB | 25.7 MiB |
| Restarts, watch errors | 0, 0 | 0, 0 |
| Incidents logged (and requests, in dry-run) | 4 | 4 |

- **The cap of 32 stays.** With 96 watches kausa used at most 25.7 MiB, about a fifth of the
  128 MiB limit, and about 12 to 13 MiB once settled. `MAX_NAMESPACES` and the chart's `$max`
  stay at 32.
- **The Deployment watches cost about what was expected.** Against the 64-watch run of
  2026-10-28, the peak rose by 3.0 MiB (22.7 to 25.7 MiB) and the steady state stayed in the
  same range (10.1 to 14.2 MiB then, 11.9 to 13.5 MiB now); 0018 expected about 4 MiB more (32
  watches of about 130 KiB, a hypothesis). The 320 Deployments of the setup never complete their
  rollout (their Pods never run), but none had waited the default 600 seconds by the end of the
  run, which took less than ten minutes from their creation, so none was past its deadline (no
  `D014` was logged) and kausa kept no record of any of them: what
  the 32 watches cost is their connections, their streams and the decoding of the list pages.
  One run per scope cannot tell 3 MiB from noise in the steady state; the peak is the number
  that decides.
- **Readiness does not wait for the namespaces one by one.** The 96 watches are polled together,
  and kausa was ready 0.6 seconds after it started, as with 64 watches.
- **The incidents are the same.** The four seeded failures gave the same four incidents and
  dry-run requests as with 64 watches, and no `D014`: `crasher` now carries a readiness gate
  nobody sets and a 20-second deadline (as in the kind test), and its `D001` was explained, so a
  `D014` for it, had it come, would have been handled. That its rollout had passed the deadline
  within the three minutes was not checked in this run; the kind test checks it.

## Setup

- kind v0.33.0 with Kubernetes v1.36.4 (the node image pinned in `.github/workflows/e2e.yml`),
  one node, a fresh cluster per scope.
- The objects of [Thousands of Pods](2026-10-28-thousands-of-pods.md), written by
  [`tests/bench/thousands-of-pods.py`](../../tests/bench/thousands-of-pods.py) with
  `32 10 3`: 32 namespaces (`scale-00` to `scale-31`), each with 10 Deployments of 3 replicas bound
  to a fake node, 960 Pods in all that never run. Then `crasher` and `failing-job` of
  `tests/e2e/failures.yaml` in `scale-00` and `scale-31`, with kausa's own image.
- kausa from `feat/v0.4-roll` after the merge of the code and chart tracks, the `runtime` image,
  installed with the chart in the repository: `dryRun=true`, `settleSeconds=10`, `rollouts` at its
  default (`true`), the default resources (limit 128 MiB), and either `rbac.scope=cluster` or
  `rbac.scope=namespaced` with the 32 namespaces (32 Roles, 32 RoleBindings, 32 `--namespace`
  arguments, nothing cluster-scoped). The `started` line said `"rollouts": true`.
- Memory: `crictl stats` inside the kind node (the container's cgroup working set), right after
  ready and every 15 seconds for three minutes; then the cgroup's `memory.peak`. Ready:
  `/readyz` polled every 0.2 seconds through the API server's Pod proxy, compared with the `ts`
  of kausa's `started` line and with the time `helm install` returned.

## Limits

- One machine and a local API server, as in the 64-watch run; the times to ready are good to
  about half a second.
- No Deployment was past its deadline, so no `DeploymentRecord` was kept: this measures the
  watches, not the records, which are kept only while a Deployment is stalled (their size was not
  measured; they hold a few strings and integers).
- The list pages were small: 10 Deployments per namespace. A page holds up to 500 whole objects
  while it is decoded, and Deployments with large annotations or scaled to zero are not bounded
  by a Pod quota (0018, "Memory"); smaller pages for all three watches are a follow-up.
- Three minutes is a steady state for watching, not a long run.
